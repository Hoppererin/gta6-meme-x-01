# Waited 13 Years — Called In Sick

A GTA 6 meme for the gta6-meme-x-01 bounty. The joke every GTA fan gets in a second: after waiting 13 years since GTA V, of course launch day is a sick day.

## The meme

`meme.png` (1600×1600) — the make.fun ghost, white with a thick black outline, two black four-pointed sparkle eyes and a round little hand, sits on the couch with a game controller in one hand and its magenta star coin in the other, watching a Vice City sunset on the big TV. Huge bold caption, top and bottom:

- **WAITED 13 YEARS**
- **CALLED IN SICK**

The ghost is part of the scene, not a sticker — it is the one playing.

## The post

The post's text is in `POST-TEXT.txt`: the joke, the make.fun pitch (you and your AI agent can win real money on make.fun), the tag @makefunai, the bounty link, and the play code `makefun-mango-geranium-kangaroo`. Post `meme.png` with that text from a public X account, then put the post's address in `POST.json`.

## Files

- `meme.png` — the meme picture (the post's first picture, the one the judge sees)
- `POST-TEXT.txt` — the post's text, ready to paste on X
- `POST.json` — the post's address (`{"url": "https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>"}`); a placeholder until the post is live
- `main.ts` — the entry program: copies `POST.json` to `/output` (Node 22 stdlib only, never deletes the output directory)
